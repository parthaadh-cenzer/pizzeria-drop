# Art direction — corrected build

The two supplied PNGs guide chunky proportions, palette, vertical depth and playful danger. Product rules determine square tile topology and initial holes independently.

Characters are original compact toy mascots: very large round heads, bean eyes and highlights, blush/smile, small bodies, mitten hands and oversized shoes. Girl has side buns/red clothing; boy has tuft/blue clothing. A shared short rig supports eight states and held-launcher IK. Review in motion and from the gameplay camera, not just a front render.

Volcano preserves animated cellular lava, tall lavafalls, varied bubbles and smoke, with enlarged crater/rim and stronger vertical features around the new floor spacing.

City is a square urban sinkhole with connected rim roads/buildings, modular window facades, scaffold bays, pipes, lights, fractured lip and a deep structural cavity. Traffic loops around the rim and one reused falling-car gag occurs infrequently. Studio front walls fade for inspection; actual gameplay restores them.

Future subjective polish should prioritize mascot silhouette, equipment readability and depth separation. Physical-phone quality tiers should be driven by profiling. Do not remove expression or structural landmarks merely to minimize polygon counts. See root BRAIN for reusable construction and optimization procedures and HANDOFF for implemented versus unverified scope.
